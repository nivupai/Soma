# Frozen nonspherical conductor experiment, v0.27.0

The complete numerical design is `src/soma/resources/nonspherical-v27.json`.
This design and its M19 seal are committed before final ensemble outcomes.
Local sealing is not external preregistration. Failures and amendments remain
visible; no tolerances, meshes or regularization are selected from localization
performance. M26 original tables, figures, measurements and protocols are immutable.

Six independent seeded synthetic heads (two prolate, two oblate, two asymmetric)
start from M26's brain/CSF/skull/scalp radii 81/82.8/87.3/90 mm and conductivities
0.33/1/0.004/0.33 S/m. Every radius surface is an axis-scaled radial direction
multiplied by exp(a ux uz + b uy³), with independent bounded axis jitter, small
nonconcentric interface offsets and a smooth localized outer-skull bump. These
are generated parameter families, never participant structural MRI.

Stored surfaces are closed and outward oriented in right-handed MRI/RAS metres.
The OpenMEEG adapter explicitly reverses face winding for its inward convention;
invalid stored normals are rejected, not repaired. Existing Soma manifold,
self-intersection, exact triangle-separation and containment checks are reused.
Minimum separation is 0.4 mm, triangle quality at least 0.2, aspect ratio at most
3.5. No invalid mesh is silently smoothed or repaired.

The nested icosphere levels 0/1/2 have 20/80/320 triangles per interface; level 3
(1280 triangles/interface) is the finest reference. Geometry and sensor projection
errors are measured separately. Thirty-two analytic scalp target directions are
mapped radially onto each discrete scalp before solving. This prescribed sensor
discretization is part of whole-model convergence, not an invisible geometry
repair. Actual and target sensor coordinates are retained. Registration models
use the declared HEAD→MRI transform and explicit projection; extra projection
beyond the frozen tolerance is a failure. True measurements and true pose remain
fixed. No shape or source is inferred from test reconstruction accuracy.

Per head, 12 independently sampled off-grid 10 nA m dipoles generate measurements
on the finest mesh. The inverse has 48 separate candidate locations and three
Cartesian components each. No measurement is generated by an inverse kernel.
One noiseless vector and eight independent vectors at each of 0.05/0.15 µV
pre-reference noise SD give 204 vectors per head, reused across models/methods.
They are not additional independent heads. Matched baseline estimates use three
mesh levels; eight prespecified sensitivity cases use level 2. Skull location,
thickness, CSF, local bone shape, sensors, registration, conductivity and the
thickness×conductivity interaction are all fixed in the design.

The existing Tikhonov/MNE/dSPM kernels use lambda2 0.001 / 1/9 / 1/9, no depth
weighting and one data-independent finest-baseline spectral scale per head.
dSPM normalized amplitudes have no physical current-bias comparison. Errors and
spread use true MRI millimetres. Resolution and amplitude normalization effects
are distinguished from anatomical mismatch. Direct comparison of headline M26
accuracy is unsupported because head/source populations and sensor discretization
differ; a historical value is never treated as a matched replication.

OpenMEEG 2.6.0 symmetric BEM is the main four-layer solver. MNE's linear-collocation
BEM independently checks matched three-layer controls, with explicitly merged
brain/CSF conductivity for that control only. Neither MNE nor its OpenMEEG wrapper
supports four layers through the standard interface. Thus the full four-layer
independent-formulation gate is NOT_ESTABLISHED unless separately demonstrated.
No FEM implementation or phantom measurement is manufactured to fill that gap.

Reference A includes the independently derived insulating homogeneous-sphere
central-dipole surface potential 3(q·u)/(4πσR²), plus MNE's heterogeneous layered
sphere approximation. Reference B requires finest-comparator relative field
error ≤5% with decreasing successive differences (ratio ≤0.8). The exact sphere
limit target is ≤2% at the finest grade. Independent matched three-layer fields
have a 10% engineering agreement target; none is a clinical accuracy threshold.
Failed reference gates are reported as numerical findings, not rescued by tuning.

The quasi-static BEM enforces potential and normal-current continuity and zero
outer-air conductivity (insulating boundary). Dipoles have zero net injected
current; average reference removes the voltage gauge. Algebraic residual ≤1e−8,
symmetry ≤1e−12 and reciprocal bilinear-solve discrepancy ≤1e−10 are checked.
These are discrete-system diagnostics, not FEM PDE residuals or measured phantom
reciprocity. Software, solver/integrator settings, hashes, coordinates and resource
use are recorded. No two wrappers of one solver are called independent physics.

Aggregate uncertainty uses a family-stratified head bootstrap followed by source
resampling within head, after averaging paired noise draws. Only six heads and
two heads/family are available: percentile intervals are conditional simulation
summaries with uncalibrated coverage, not human-population confidence intervals.
Missing cases remain incomplete. Every source/method row is accounted for.

Budget: 240 s per solver, at most 7000 dense unknowns / 2 GiB estimated dense
storage, 1800 s total. If infeasible, amend the protocol before final accuracy
inspection; do not silently omit expensive cases. Ordinary CI runs only small
physical references and adversarial cases. PHANTOM_NOT_AVAILABLE; FEM_NOT_PERFORMED.

## Revision 2, before final outcome inspection

Revision 1 is retained at commit 3687ced. A pure geometry/source-margin check found
one 60 mm candidate only 1.63 mm from the coarsest oblate boundary. Revision 2
uses the same 35/55 mm candidate shells for every head, leaving the 2 mm margin
criterion unchanged and retaining all six heads. No localization or amplitude
performance was inspected to make this amendment. Original resource/seal files
remain intact; active files have the `-r2` suffix, with explicit M19 parent links.
A new M27 spherical control uses the same grid and per-head source/noise schedule.
It is separate from immutable M26 measurements and does not turn unmatched M26
headline accuracy into a directly comparable value. Source/noise/head seed
schedules and the signed warp equation are explicit in the amended resource.
