# Anatomical generalization and statistical units

M11's previously examined BrainWeb normal and Mindboggle NKI-RS-22-11 remain
**development** evidence. They are not new holdouts, tissue truth substitutes, or
skull/scalp standards. The fresh IBSR split was frozen before label inspection;
registered access prevented acquisition. No genuine prospective human tissue
scores or cohort generalization are reported. The reusable cohort engine and its
synthetic analytical tests do not fill that evidence gap.

The SCI head is one computational anatomy with unresolved label frame/units and
ID ontology. Its T1 and segmentation integrity/connectivity are inspected, but
that is not an independent manual tissue benchmark. Unrelated tetrahedral mesh
validity cannot establish three-layer BEM readiness. Single annotated anatomy
would still lack inter-rater and population uncertainty if alignment were resolved.

Synthetic conductor experiments use validated known spheres and independently
recomputed MNE analytical/BEM leads. Radius +1 mm and skull conductivity half are
known model changes; they are not distributions of biological variation. Source
positions, sensors and orientation basis stay corresponding. Priors/regularizers
are compared at all prespecified levels without choosing a winner using truth.

Three source depths and 0°/30° orientations are deterministic configurations.
Twenty matched projected Gaussian draws produce paired noise comparisons and
95% t intervals conditional on those configurations. Correlated two-source signals
have 10 Hz waveforms, 100 Hz sampling, one second duration and correlation .8.
These multiple-source cases use a matched full time series; no per-source isolated
attribution from a single global peak is claimed. Fit-noise diagonal mismatch is
a sensitivity condition, not human measurement uncertainty estimated from data.

Primary contrast is normalized-minus-raw uniform-prior error/amplitude. Other
prior/model/regularization contrasts are exploratory, descriptive sensitivity;
there are no multiple-comparison significance claims. Boundary and discretization
interactions are not added as a biological error budget. Human intervals, when
available, use subjects as units; no thousands-of-simulations-as-subjects claim.

Acceptance distinguishes computational reference inspection, numerical source-prior
validation, human tissue-reference evaluation, external conductor validation and
population generalization. The latter three remain incomplete here. Proposed M12:
authorized subject-disjoint tissue references, documented SCI label/frame chains,
verified conductor interfaces, real cortical quadrature and prospectively specified
regularization conventions. M12 has not been implemented.
