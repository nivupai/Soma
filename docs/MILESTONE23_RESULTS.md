## Domain shift, uncertainty and unresolved objections

Observed human shift is metadata only:250 versus160Hz,22 versus64 EEG reference
pool, hardware filtering and cue origin differ. No physiological explanation for an
unobserved performance difference is inferred. Human confidence intervals are absent.
For permitted future outcomes, the independent resampling unit is the participant;
small N4/N9 conditional bootstrap intervals cannot establish broad population precision.
No trial-level pseudo-replication or unsupported human permutation p-value is allowed.

The separate [methodological challenge audit](EXTERNAL_EEG_STATISTICAL_ANALYSIS.md)
leaves model fidelity, aggregate-export interpretation, actual data acquisition,
real-adapter empirical validation, small-N precision and independent human peer review
unresolved. It is not external preregistration or independent human peer review.
A newly reconstructed source fit would require explicit labeling, archiving and a
new plan; it cannot backdate the missing M22 artifact.

M22's60.77% band-power transfer score remains historical EEGBCI reproduction; it is
not assigned to this external candidate. No human performance improvement or
external generalization claim is supported. The scientific result is that the
requested original-model experiment cannot currently be executed faithfully.
No Milestone24 work was started.
